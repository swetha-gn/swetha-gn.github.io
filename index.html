What I work on

Where machine learning meets systems, performance and physical reality.

Hardware-aware deep learning

Quantizing neural networks to INT8 and INT6 and testing them with hardware in the loop, so models fit the power, memory and precision limits of photonic chips and AI accelerators. Try it: fewer bits, smaller model, more error.

Digital twins & surrogate models

Neural surrogates trained on TCAD process-simulation data that predict mechanical stress across device designs in milliseconds. A deep ensemble attaches an uncertainty to every prediction, flagging when to fall back to the full simulation.

Surrogate MLTCADUncertainty

Next: physics-informed models

Physics-informed neural networks put the governing equations into the training loss, so predictions stay physically consistent with less simulation data. Next steps: coupling stress, thermal and optical physics, and extending to waveguides, resonators and photonic ICs.
